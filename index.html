Abstract

3D scene diffusion models are trained on furnished-room layouts whose geometry encodes simple physical regularities. We ask not merely whether such regularities are present in the model, but where they are exposed in the reverse denoising process. We compare three candidate internal readouts: passive object-marginal uncertainty, branch risk, and local denoising response. The result is a sharp separation. For the tested 3D scene violations, empirical marginal/branch variance and branch risk are not reliable or parameterization-stable physical plausibility readouts, and the tested first-order relational readouts do not yield practical invalid-vs-control readouts for collision or scale-overlap.

In contrast, floor support is exposed directly by the deterministic low-noise x̂0 field: under controlled support stress, x̂0 behaves like a learned local projector onto the empirical support manifold g = cy − h/2 − yfloor = 0. Across bedroom, livingroom, and diningroom DiffuScene checkpoints, including strict-valid layouts sampled from the model itself, the response localizes the perturbed object, aligns with the repair direction, scales with violation severity, and follows Δg ≈ −λt g with λt ≈ 1. These findings give a limited but actionable taxonomy of implicit scene physics: representation-aligned object-local support appears as score-field geometry, while empirical variance, branch risk, and tested first-order relational readouts do not yield a general physics oracle.

The Low-Noise x̂0 Field Contracts Toward the Support Manifold

(a) Floor-support coordinate g = cy − h/2 − yfloor; normal lifts move objects off the manifold (g > 0), tangent controls preserve g = 0, downward penetration tests the opposite sign. (b) Processed 3D-FRONT layouts concentrate sharply near g = 0 across bedroom, livingroom, and diningroom; legend reports the fraction of non-wall-mounted objects with |g| ≤ 1 cm. (c) Under controlled support stress, the single-step x̂0 response follows Δg ≈ −λt g: floating gaps are pulled down, penetration gaps are pushed up, and valid controls stay near zero. (d) The fitted contraction coefficient λt stays near the ideal projector value of 1 in the low-noise regime across room types.

Takeaway. Floor support is exposed as score-field geometry — the low-noise denoising field acts like a learned local projector onto the empirical support manifold.

Matched Controls Rule Out the Simplest Confounds

At representative low-noise time t = 25, normal/tangent controls, downward penetration, and a model-space norm-matched horizontal control all preserve strong response, support-normal repair, localization, and severity monotonicity. The tangent condition rules out a pure vertical-coordinate prior; the downward condition verifies the field can push upward when g < 0; the model-space control separates support repair from generic perturbation magnitude.

Variance and Branch Risk Are Not the Carrier

Curves average matched support-control families. The x̂0 support-normal repair readout remains saturated in the low-noise regime, while window and final branch variance stay near 0.5 (paired chance). Branch target-risk reaches only 0.000–0.028 PairAcc in matched runs.

Takeaway. The physical signal is the deterministic denoising mean field / x̂0, not sampled trajectory variance or branch risk. Empirical uncertainty is not a general physics oracle in this setting.

Replicates on the Model's Own Generated Layouts

The support-normal contraction also appears around strict-valid layouts sampled from the model itself. Across generated bedroom, livingroom, and diningroom layouts, the low-noise x̂0 response remains close to the ideal projector. (Generated layouts have no original 3D-FRONT room mask, so strict-valid filtering uses a rectangular-room fallback; this is a neighborhood check around model samples, not a replacement for real room-mask evaluation.)

The Support Manifold Is Sharp Empirically

Empirical bottom-gap g distribution on processed 3D-FRONT bedroom layouts. Mass concentrates within 1 cm of zero, consistent with a sharp data manifold and with the p(g)-Gaussian local-score interpretation of the contraction law.

Contributions

  • C1. A support-manifold repair field: floor support is exposed as a low-noise x̂0 support-normal contraction field, Δg ≈ −λt g, on processed and generated DiffuScene layouts.
  • C2. Counterfactual support stress tests (normal/tangent, downward penetration, model-space norm control) separate support repair from vertical-OOD bias, one-way downward pulls, reconstruction bias, and generic perturbation magnitude.
  • C3. Readout separation: passive marginal variance, branch variance, and branch risk do not carry the controlled support signal; the deterministic x̂0 denoising field does. Tested first-order relational readouts also fail for collision and scale-overlap.
  • C4. Boundary taxonomy: implicit scene physics is structured and limited, not a universal denoising oracle — choose denoising-field diagnostics for representation-aligned constraints, and keep external geometry / simulation / collision-detection for relational and mesh-level constraints.
